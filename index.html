What a pause does and does not do

  • Blocks new entrants only. ETH already committed and quota already granted are untouched.
  • Does not reach a project that has already launched. Trading on a launched pool continues.
  • Cannot move your funds. The treasury has no withdraw, sweep, rescue or delegatecall path in its bytecode. Its only exit burns to 0xdead, and that address is hardcoded.
  • You do not have to take our word for any of this — the factory is linked above and verified on the explorer.
